Cribbage Squares

Out now on Google Play

A solo puzzle built on cribbage scoring. Place 16 cards in a 4×4 square so that its four rows and four columns, each scored as a hand with the shared starter, add up to the level's target.

Free on Android.

Get it on Google Play

How a round works

  1. A starter is turned up

    It counts in all eight lines, so it shapes every decision you make.

  2. Cards arrive one at a time

    You can see the card coming up next and the one after it.

  3. Tap a space to place each card

    Row and column scores update as you go. You have three undos per level.

  4. Fill the square and reach the target

    When the last card goes down, all eight lines are added up. Reach the level's target to clear it.

How a line scores

Standard cribbage hand scoring. Each row and each column is scored together with the starter, so a single card can score in two hands at once.

  • Fifteen

    2 each

    Any group of cards that adds up to 15. Jacks, queens and kings count 10, aces count 1.

    K5

  • Pair

    2 each

    Two cards of the same rank. Three of a kind makes three pairs, four makes six.

    22

  • Run

    1 per card

    Three or more ranks in a row. A duplicated rank doubles the run.

    345

  • Flush

    4, or 5

    All four cards in the line share a suit. One more point if the starter matches.

    2710K

  • Nobs

    1

    A jack in the line of the same suit as the starter.

    Jwith a heart starter

What's in the game

  • 600 levels in three tiers: Normal, Hard and Expert, 200 each. Clear five levels to open the next tier.
  • Seeds. Play the next level, a random one, or type in a seed to replay a specific deal.
  • Line breakdowns. Tap the score beside any row or column to see how it was counted.
  • Three undos per level.
  • 16 languages.
  • No account. Progress and settings are saved on your phone.
  • Stuck? Watch an optional ad to see the level's best layout. Nothing else in the game asks you to watch one.
