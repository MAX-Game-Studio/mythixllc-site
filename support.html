Cribbage Squares questions

Where can I get Cribbage Squares?

It's free for Android phones and tablets.Get it on Google Play.

Do I need an account?

No. Progress and preferences are stored locally on your device, and there is no Mythix account or cloud sign-in.

How do I reset my progress?

Because progress is stored locally, clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it may remove your progress permanently. Back up anything important before doing so.

Can I change the language or sound settings?

Yes. Open Settings in the game to choose a supported language and adjust sound preferences.

Are rewarded ads required?

No. The rewarded video is optional and is only used to reveal a round's best layout. You can continue playing without using it.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, open Settings in the game and tap Privacy choices. Everywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

What should I include in a bug report?

Tell us your device model, operating-system version, app version, language, and the steps that caused the issue. A screenshot is helpful when available.

Email support

Tell us the game, your phone model, the app version and what happened. Please don't send passwords or other sensitive information.
