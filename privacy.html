Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Mythix LLC website at mythixllc.com and the mobile game Cribbage Squares. “Mythix,” “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to Mythix LLC.

2. Information stored on your device

Cribbage Squares stores game progress, completed levels, and preferences such as language and sound settings locally on your device. The game does not require an account, and Mythix does not receive this local game data. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app may permanently remove it.

3. Information you send us

If you email us for support, privacy questions, or business inquiries, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, message, and attachments. We use it to respond, troubleshoot, maintain security, and meet legal obligations. We retain correspondence only as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes.

4. Website and hosting data

As of the effective date, this website does not use advertising cookies or first-party analytics. Our hosting provider may process standard technical logs—such as IP address, browser or device type, requested page, and timestamp—to deliver, secure, and maintain the site.

5. Rewarded advertising

Cribbage Squares uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to offer an optional rewarded video that reveals a round's best layout. The SDK starts when the game launches, after any consent required in your region, and loads an ad in advance. Because of this, Google may automatically collect or process the following information even if you never choose to watch an ad:

  • IP address and approximate location;
  • app interactions, including launches, taps, and ad views;
  • diagnostic and performance information;
  • device, app, or advertising identifiers; and
  • advertising and fraud-prevention information.

Google may use this information to provide and measure ads, prevent fraud and abuse, maintain security, and support analytics. Processing may occur in countries other than your own. Learn more in Google's partner-sites policy, privacy policy, and advertising policy.

6. Consent and choices

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent message before any ads are requested. You can consent, decline, or choose specific purposes, and you can change your choice at any time from the game's Settings (⚙) → Privacy choices. Watching a rewarded ad is always optional: you can decline it and keep playing. Your device settings also let you reset or delete your advertising identifier.

7. Children

Cribbage Squares is intended for adults and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review and delete it when appropriate.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing of your personal information. You may also have the right to appeal a decision or contact a local data-protection authority. Email us to make a request; we may need to verify it before responding.

9. Security and changes

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no transmission or storage system is completely secure. We may update this policy as the game, service providers, or legal requirements change. The effective date above will identify the latest version.

10. Contact us

Privacy questions and requests: privacy@mythixllc.com
Game support: support@mythixllc.com
Mythix LLC, Texas, United States

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 30 日

1. 适用范围

本隐私政策适用于 Mythix LLC 位于 mythixllc.com 的网站和手机游戏 Cribbage Squares。文中的“我们”指 Mythix LLC。

2. 保存在您设备上的信息

Cribbage Squares 会将游戏进度、已完成关卡以及语言、声音等偏好设置保存在您的设备本地。游戏不要求创建账户，Mythix 不会收到这些本地游戏数据。清除应用数据或卸载应用可能会永久删除这些信息。

3. 您主动发送给我们的信息

如果您通过电子邮件联系我们以寻求支持、咨询隐私问题或开展商务合作，我们会收到您选择提供的信息，例如邮箱地址、邮件内容和附件。我们仅将这些信息用于回复、排查问题、保障安全及履行法律义务，并只在实现上述目的所需的合理期限内保存。

4. 网站和托管数据

截至本政策生效日，本网站不使用广告 Cookie，也未启用第一方分析工具。为提供、保护和维护网站，托管服务商可能会处理标准技术日志，例如 IP 地址、浏览器或设备类型、访问页面和时间戳。

5. 激励广告

Cribbage Squares 使用 Google Mobile Ads SDK（AdMob）提供可选的激励视频，看完后会显示本局的最佳布局。游戏启动时，该 SDK 会在您所在地区要求的同意步骤完成后启动，并预先加载一条广告。因此，即使您从未选择观看广告，Google 也可能自动收集或处理以下信息：

  • IP 地址和大致位置；
  • 应用交互信息，包括启动、点击和广告观看；
  • 诊断和性能信息；
  • 设备、应用或广告标识符；
  • 广告及反欺诈相关信息。

Google 可能将这些信息用于提供和衡量广告、防止欺诈和滥用、维护安全以及支持分析。信息可能在您所在国家或地区之外处理。详情请参阅 Google 的合作伙伴网站政策、隐私政策和广告政策。

6. 同意和选择

如果您位于欧洲经济区、英国或瑞士，游戏会在请求任何广告之前显示 Google 的同意消息。您可以同意、拒绝或按用途分别选择，之后也可以随时在游戏的“设置（⚙）→ 隐私选项”中更改。观看激励广告始终是可选的：您可以拒绝并继续游戏。设备设置也允许您重置或删除广告标识符。

7. 儿童隐私

Cribbage Squares 面向成年人，并非面向儿童。我们不会故意收集 13 岁以下儿童的个人信息。如果您认为有儿童向我们提供了个人信息，请联系我们，我们会进行核查并在适当情况下删除。

8. 您的隐私权利

根据您所在地区的法律，您可能有权访问、更正、删除、限制处理或反对处理您的个人信息，也可能有权提出申诉或联系当地数据保护机构。您可以通过电子邮件提出请求；我们可能需要先验证请求者身份。

9. 安全和政策更新

我们采取合理的管理和技术措施保护信息，但任何传输或存储系统都无法保证绝对安全。随着游戏、服务商或法律要求变化，我们可能更新本政策；页面顶部的生效日期将显示最新版本。

游戏支持：support@mythixllc.com
